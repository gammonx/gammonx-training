White bias is the concern. The mean label went from 0.615 → 0.645 despite randomizing starting color. Starting color randomization should keep it near 0.50–0.55. The fact it's still drifting upward means the neural net has learned an asymmetric playing style that systematically favors white positions. This will compound if left uncorrected — gen4 data generated by gen3 will be even more biased.
